交通方式Getting Around

  • 在飯店附近租借125cc機車，需持有國際駕照或台灣駕照。Rent a 125cc scooter near the hotel — international driving permit or Taiwan license required.

Practical Info

Jibei Island Tips

  • Wear water shoes — the sandbar can have sharp coral underfoot.
  • Apply sunscreen before boarding; no shade on the open sandbar or ferry deck.
  • Bring a dry bag — spray is common on the ferry crossing.
  • 目斗嶼 landing may be cancelled in rough seas — check with the operator on the morning.

Night Squid Fishing Tips

  • Wear a light jacket — it gets cool on the water after dark even in September.
  • Motion sickness tablets recommended if prone to seasickness.
  • The crew will gut and bag your catch — you can keep what you catch.
  • Confirm meeting point: 南海遊客中心 (Nanhai Visitor Centre), Magong.

Highlights of the Southern Four Islands

東吉島 · Dongji Island

  • A near-deserted fishing island — Qing-era stone houses slowly reclaimed by nature.
  • Panoramic ocean views from the Japanese-era lighthouse at the island's peak.
  • Crystal-clear waters ideal for snorkelling off the rocky shore.

藍洞 · Blue Cave (西吉)

  • Bioluminescent blue glow caused by noctiluca algae in the cave's still water.
  • Entry typically by small rubber dinghy or via snorkelling — the guide leads the way.
  • Light conditions matter — overcast days can reduce the glow effect.

七美島 · Qimei Island

  • 心形礁 (heart-shaped reef) — iconic Penghu landmark, best viewed from the clifftop overlook.
  • 雙心石滬 (double-heart stone weir) — ancient fish trap built from coral stones, visible from the shoreline.
  • Scooter included in the Klook package for island exploration.

Day 3 Tips

  • Arrive at 南海遊客中心 by 07:20 — the boat will not wait. Set an early alarm.
  • Bring motion sickness tablets — the open-water crossing to the southern islands can be rough.
  • Wear layers; wind on deck can be cold in the early morning even in September.
  • Camera essential — 藍洞 and 心形礁 are among Penghu's most spectacular sights.
