Petrol Tips in Japan
  • Look for セルフ (Serufu) for self-service stations — cheaper and easy to use.
  • Nozzle colours: Red = Regular (レギュラー)  ·  Green = Diesel (軽油)  ·  Yellow = High Octane (ハイオク)
  • Full tank: 「満タンでお願いします」 — Mantan de onegai shimasu
  • Payment via touchscreen first, then cash or credit card — pay before pumping.
  • Touch the grounding panel (metal pad on pump) before fuelling to discharge static.

Rinku Premium Outlets 臨空城暢貨中心
One of Japan's largest outlet malls with 210+ stores spanning luxury brands like Dolce & Gabbana and Kate Spade to Nike and Adidas. Tax-free shopping is available — bring your passport for savings of up to 10%. A perfect first stop right after landing.
日本最大型的暢貨中心之一，匯聚逾210間店鋪，涵蓋Dolce & Gabbana、Kate Spade等奢侈品牌，以及Nike、Adidas等運動品牌。持護照可享免稅優惠，最高節省10%。甫抵達後的第一站首選。
Shopping Tax-Free Open 10:00–20:00
© Wikimedia Commons
Rinku Gate Tower & Ferris Wheel 臨空閘塔及摩天輪
The landmark twin towers and the 85-metre Rinku No Hoshi Ferris Wheel define the Rinku skyline. A ride on the Ferris wheel rewards you with panoramic views over Osaka Bay, Kansai Airport, and on a clear day, as far as Awaji Island to the south.
標誌性的雙子塔與高達85米的「臨空之星」摩天輪，是臨空城最具代表性的地標。乘坐摩天輪可俯瞰大阪灣及關西國際機場全景，晴天時甚至能遠眺南方的淡路島。
Landmark Observation Paid Entry

Byōdō-in Temple 平等院
A UNESCO World Heritage Site founded in 1052, Byōdō-in is home to the iconic Phoenix Hall (Hōō-dō) — a masterpiece of Heian-period aristocratic architecture reflected in a serene garden pond. Its image appears on the Japanese 10-yen coin.
平等院建於1052年（平安時代），是聯合國教科文組織世界文化遺產。以著名的鳳凰堂聞名，此建築凌駕於倒影池上，是日本貴族建築瑰寶，其圖像更印於日本十円硬幣之上。
UNESCO Heritage Temple Open 08:30–17:30
© Wikimedia Commons
Uji Tea Houses 宇治茶屋
Uji is Japan's most prized tea region. Nakamura Tokichi Honten (est. 1854) serves matcha sweets in a heritage tea-factory café. Tsujiri Uji Main Store and the ancient Tsuen Main Branch complete the trio — all within a short riverside walk of each other.
宇治是日本最負盛名的茶葉產區。中村藤吉本店（創立於1854年）以古樸茶廠改建的咖啡廳供應精緻抹茶甜點；辻利宇治本店及歷史悠久的通園本店同樣提供精選茶飲與和菓子，各店沿河步行皆可輕鬆到達。
Matcha Tea Culture Historic Cafés
© Wikimedia Commons
Tachibana Bridge 橘橋
A charming footbridge spanning the Uji River near Byōdō-in, Tachibana Bridge offers picturesque views of the river and the wooded temple grounds beyond. A leisurely riverside stroll between the bridge and the temple is the perfect way to round off the afternoon in Uji.
橘橋是橫跨宇治川的古雅小橋，毗鄰平等院，可眺望川面及山林中的寺院景色。漫步橘橋與平等院之間的河畔步道，是在宇治度過午後時光的最悠然方式。
Scenic Riverside Walk Free
Meals

🍱 Lunch — Irori Beniya

  • Irori Beniya is a beloved traditional restaurant in Uji serving charcoal-grilled fish dishes cooked over an irori (sunken hearth). Signature dishes include grilled Atka mackerel set meals and grilled salmon with salmon roe rice. Rated 5.0 on Tripadvisor.

🥩 Dinner — Matsukiya Main Store  近江牛專門餐廳松喜屋本店

  • Matsukiya is a legendary Omi beef (近江牛) specialist run by the same family since the Meiji era. The main store sits on the bank of the Seta River in Otsu — perfectly placed en route to Lake Biwa. Enjoy premium cuts prepared as sukiyaki, shabu-shabu, or steak, finished with the chef's signature bubble salt.

La Collina Ōmi-Hachiman 拉科利納近江八幡
A magical complex by confectionery group Taneya, centred on a grass-thatched main building designed by architect Terunobu Fujimori. Home to Club Harie — Japan's premier Baumkuchen brand — and the Taneya wagashi shop. Shiga's top tourist facility for nine consecutive years.
由糖果集團TANEYA打造的夢幻園區，以建築師藤森照信設計的茅草屋頂主建築為核心，四周環抱翠綠山丘，宛如童話世界。設有日本頂級年輪蛋糕品牌Club Harie及和菓子店TANEYA，連續九年榮登滋賀縣人氣旅遊設施榜首。
Sweets & Café Architecture Open 09:00–18:00

Hachiman-bori Boat Tour 八幡堀遊船
A 35-minute boat journey through Ōmi-Hachiman's historic canal, guided by skilled boatmen with traditional oars. The waterway is flanked by 400-year-old white-walled merchant storehouses and willows — a scene straight out of a period drama. Both rowing and engine-powered boats available.
由技術嫻熟的船夫以傳統木槳引領，全程約35分鐘，穿梭於近江八幡歷史悠久的運河之中。兩岸矗立逾400年歷史的白牆商家倉庫與垂柳，景色宛如走入時代劇場景。可選擇人力划船或機動船。
Boat Tour 35 min Historic Canal
© Wikimedia Commons
Himure Hachimangu & Omi Merchant Cityscape 日牟禮八幡宮及近江商人街道
Himure Hachimangu Shrine, guardian deity of the Ōmi merchants for over 1,000 years, sits amid ancient trees at the foot of Mt. Hachiman. Nearby, the Omi Merchant Cityscape — a nationally designated preservation district — preserves 2 km of Edo-to-Meiji merchant residences and storehouses.
日牟禮八幡宮擁有逾千年歷史，長期作為近江商人的守護神，坐落於八幡山麓古木之間。附近的近江商人街道全長約2公里，被列為國家重要傳統建造物群保存地區，保存著江戶至明治時代的商家建築，是日本最完整的商家街景之一。
Shrine Historic District Free
Meals

🍱 Lunch — Eel Hitsumabushi Sumiyagura

  • Sumiyagura specialises in hitsumabushi — grilled freshwater eel over rice served in a wooden tub, enjoyed three ways: plain, with condiments, or as ochazuke with dashi broth. The eel is grilled over premium binchotan charcoal. Their signature double hitsumabushi adds Omi beef for an indulgent combination.

🍽 Dinner — PIERI MORIYAMA  PIERI守山

  • Dinner at one of the restaurants in PIERI MORIYAMA, a lakeside shopping mall in Moriyama, Shiga, situated on the shores of Lake Biwa. The dining floor offers a variety of Japanese and international options, many with views over the lake — a scenic end to the day before heading to the hotel.

Mangetsu-ji Uki-midō 滿月寺浮御堂
A small Buddhist hall perched on stilts over Lake Biwa in Katata, originally built in the 11th century by the monk Genshin to protect fishermen and sailors. One of Lake Biwa's most iconic and photographed sights — serene and magical at dawn when perfectly reflected in the still water.
位於大津市堅田的浮御堂，是建於木樁之上、漂浮於琵琶湖水面的小型佛堂，相傳由11世紀源信和尚修建，以庇護湖上漁民及舟人。為琵琶湖最具代表性的地標之一，倒映湖面的景色尤為迷人，日出時分前往更是如夢似幻。
Temple Lake Biwa Iconic View

Shirahige Shrine 白鬚神社
One of Japan's oldest shrines, famous for its dramatic torii gate rising from the waters of Lake Biwa — often called the "Torii of Lake Biwa" and likened to Miyajima's iconic gate. Sunrise visits are especially rewarding as the gate glows against the morning light reflected on the lake.
日本歷史最悠久的神社之一，以矗立於琵琶湖湖面的鳥居聞名，被譽為「琵琶湖的大鳥居」，常與廣島宮島的著名鳥居相提並論。日出時分前往尤為推薦，朝陽映照下鳥居與湖面交相輝映，景色震撼人心。
Shrine Lake Torii Sunrise Spot

Enryaku-ji 延曆寺
A UNESCO World Heritage Site founded in 788 by the monk Saicho atop Mt. Hiei. Headquarters of Japanese Tendai Buddhism for over 1,200 years, its vast complex spans three areas across the mountaintop. The founding monks of Zen, Pure Land, and other major Buddhist schools all trained here.
延曆寺建於788年，由最澄上人創立於比叡山頂，是聯合國教科文組織世界文化遺產，天台宗總本山。寺院建築群分布山頂三個區域，逾千二百年來影響日本佛教深遠，禪宗、淨土宗等多個主要宗派的開山祖師皆出身於此。
UNESCO Heritage Mt. Hiei Open 08:30–16:30

Kayabuki-no-Sato 茅葺之里
A nationally designated preservation district in Miyama, Kyoto Prefecture, Kayabuki-no-Sato is a serene village of some 38 beautifully preserved thatched-roof farmhouses. Set against forested mountains with a river running through, it is one of Japan's most photogenic rural landscapes — utterly timeless and a world away from city life.
茅葺之里位於京都府南丹市美山町，是國家重要傳統建造物群保存地區，保存著約38棟美麗的茅草屋頂農家。村莊依山傍水，景色如畫，是日本最具代表性的農村風光之一，寧靜悠然，令人彷彿穿越時空。
Historic Village Thatched Roofs Free Entry
© visitjapan-vegetarian.com
Yurakawa Railway Bridge 由良川鐵橋
The Kitakinki Tango Railway sweeps across the Yura River on a long iron bridge near Miyazu. At high tide the bridge appears to skim just above the water, creating the magical illusion of a train gliding over the sea — earning it the nickname "sea train." One of the most iconic railway photography spots in the Kyoto-Tango region.
北近畿丹後鐵道橫跨由良川的長形鐵橋，位於宮津附近。漲潮時橋面幾乎與水面齊平，列車仿佛直接在海面上行駛，因而得名「海上列車」，是京都丹後地區最具代表性的鐵道攝影聖地之一。
Scenic Railway Sea Train Photography Spot

Ine Bay & Bay Cruise 伊根灣及遊覽船
A UNESCO-nominated fishing village famous for 230+ funaya — traditional fishermen's dwellings built directly over the water, with boat garages on the ground floor. The 30-min bay cruise weaves among the funaya with seagulls in tow, framed by forested mountains.
伊根灣面臨申遺，以逾230棟「舟屋」（funaya）聞名——漁民傳統住居的地面層即為停泊漁船的船庫。約30分鐘的遊覽船穿梭舟屋之間，海鷗伴飛，四周青山環抱，景色怡人。
Boat Cruise Funaya 30 min
© Navitime
Amanohashidate Viewland 天橋立觀景樂園
Ride the chairlift up for the famous matanozoki — bending over to view the sandbar upside-down, making it appear to float in the sky like a bridge to heaven. The park also features panoramic views of Miyazu Bay, a zip line, and rides for all ages.
乘坐纜椅上山，體驗著名的「股窺」（matanozoki）——彎腰倒看沙洲，宛如沙洲懸浮天際。園內另設宮津灣全景觀景台、飛索及各類遊樂設施。
Chairlift Panoramic View Matanozoki

Amanohashidate Sandbar 天橋立砂洲
One of Japan's Three Views (日本三景), this 3.6-km pine-covered sandbar stretches across Miyazu Bay. Stroll or hire a bicycle along the path flanked by ancient pines with the sea shimmering on both sides. Said to resemble a bridge reaching up to heaven.
天橋立是「日本三景」之一，橫跨宮津灣、長達3.6公里的翠松沙洲。沿沙洲步行或騎單車，兩側海水波光粼粼，古松林立，景色令人難忘，相傳其形如通往天界之橋。
Japan's Three Views Walk / Cycle Free

🍽 Dinner — Nitanda

Kinosaki Marine World 城崎海洋世界
A marine theme park on the Sea of Japan coast near Kinosaki Onsen, featuring live dolphin and sea lion shows, penguin parades, and a wide variety of ocean creatures. The open-air performance stage overlooking the Sea of Japan provides a spectacular natural backdrop for the shows.
位於日本海沿岸、城崎溫泉附近的海洋主題公園，設有海豚及海獅表演、企鵝遊行及各類海洋生物展示。面向日本海的露天表演舞台以大海為背景，景色壯觀，令人難忘。
Marine Park Dolphin Show Sea of Japan

Asanoya & Yumura Onsen 朝野家及湯村溫泉
A beloved traditional ryokan set beside the river in Yumura Onsen, one of Hyogo's most celebrated hot spring villages. Yumura's Arasawa spring reaches 98°C — among Japan's hottest natural springs. Unwind in indoor and outdoor baths and enjoy a multi-course kaiseki dinner served in the room.
朝野家是湯村溫泉依河而建的傳統日式旅館，湯村溫泉的荒湯泉水溫高達攝氏98度，是日本溫度最高的天然溫泉之一。旅館設有室內外溫泉浴池，並提供精緻懷石晚餐，在客房內享用，悠然自得。
Ryokan Hot Spring Dinner & Breakfast
Meals

🐬 Lunch — Kinosaki Marine World Terrace

🍱 Dinner — Asanoya Ryokan  朝野家旅館

  • Dinner is included with the stay at Asanoya — a traditional kaiseki multi-course meal served in-room, featuring fresh seasonal ingredients and local Hyogo specialities. A perfect way to end the day after the hot spring baths.

Himeji Castle 姬路城
Japan's finest surviving feudal castle, designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site and National Treasure. Known as "Shirasagi-jō" (White Heron Castle) for its elegant white plastered walls, the complex comprises 83 buildings across multiple baileys connected by winding paths designed to confuse invaders. The main keep stands six stories tall and offers sweeping views over Himeji city.
日本保存最完整的封建城堡，亦是聯合國教科文組織世界文化遺產及國寶。因其優雅的白色灰泥外牆，被譽為「白鷺城」。城郭由83棟建築組成，通過曲折迴廊相連，設計巧妙以迷惑入侵者。天守閣高達六層，可俯瞰整個姬路市全景。

Kokoromi 心見
A curated dessert and sweets boutique located inside VIERRA Himeji, the stylish shopping complex adjacent to Himeji Station. Kokoromi specialises in premium Japanese confections that highlight local Hyogo ingredients, offering an elegant selection of seasonal treats, wagashi, and gift sets — perfect for picking up edible souvenirs before heading to Okayama.
位於姬路車站旁 VIERRA Himeji 購物中心內的精選甜品與和菓子專賣店。心見精心挑選以兵庫縣當地食材製作的高品質日式點心，提供琳瑯滿目的季節限定甜品、和菓子及精美禮盒，是離開姬路前採購伴手禮的最佳去處。

🏯 Lunch — Kassui-ken 活水軒

  • 活水軒 (Kassui-ken) is a historic restaurant nestled within the grounds of Himeji Castle, offering traditional Japanese cuisine with a view of the castle's elegant architecture. Enjoy set lunches featuring seasonal dishes in a refined tatami-style setting — a rare chance to dine inside one of Japan's most celebrated castle complexes.

🐟 Dinner — Uoya Taisho

  • Uoya Taisho is a well-regarded seafood restaurant offering an extensive menu of fresh fish dishes, sashimi, and grilled seafood. A great choice for ending the day with the freshest catches from the Seto Inland Sea, prepared in classic Japanese style.
